and rules that hold

The ledger is the single source of truth.

Reports, tax workpapers and practice billing read from the double-entry general ledger and say which figures are actual, forecast, budget or scenario.

Every change leaves a trail.

Imports, approvals, overrides and write-offs are recorded in an audit log, and sensitive identifiers such as tax numbers are stored sealed and revealed only with permission.

Organisations are kept apart.

Data is isolated by organisation, and inside a practice by client, so a team member only ever sees the clients they are assigned.
